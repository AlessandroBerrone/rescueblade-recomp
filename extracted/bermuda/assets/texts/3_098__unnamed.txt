DAY 3 Monster Movie Mayhem
Things are really going crazy in the Bermuda Triangle. Several  ferocious monsters of the deep have been sighted off the resort's coast!

Fly out and douse the burning pleasure vessels and rescue the crews. Then ferry them to the Hotel.

Dodge the fearsome flaming flumes of fire! Can you find a way to fight back against these  mad, malevolent monstrosities? Force feed the Bodzillas some water to choke them mid flame blast.

TIP: Bodzillas hate coconut concussion!
