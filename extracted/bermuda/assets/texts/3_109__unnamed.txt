DAY 4 Submarine Cash Dash
No fair! cableGuy has spent his whole holiday rescuing  other people and has run out of holiday money. Help cableGuy plunge beneath the waves and go in search of sunken treasure.

If you see any of the famous missing Flight 19 plane-wrecks down there, make sure you take some pictures. They could be worth something! The closer you are, the more your pictures will be worth. cableGuy's film has 12 frames, so try to take 2 good shots per plane.

HOLD DOWN SPACEBAR to photograph each wreck.

WARNING : Dodge the hungry Blue Marlin Fish!
