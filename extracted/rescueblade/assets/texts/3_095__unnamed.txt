MISSION 001  Helipads & Bell Buoys

Use the CURSOR KEYS to fly your helicopter around  the  islands. Press P to pause.

Gently land on each of the Helipads. 

Hover above each Bell Buoy to ring the bell.

Don't land too heavily or you will damage your helicopter. Complete the level at speed to earn a massive Time Bonus!

TOP TIP: Dab the keys to achieve smooth throttle and pitch control, and avoid  altitude oscillation.
