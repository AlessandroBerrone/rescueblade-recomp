MISSION 002 Introducing Cable Guy
Use spacebar to toggle the winch up / down.

Fly around  the  islands and hover above each pickup,  tap the spacebar to lower your cable guy down to grab the item. Tap the Spacebar again to haul up the goodies.


FUEL BARREL:				Raises fuel level

TOOL BOX :				Reduces damage level

TREASURE :				Huge points bonus

SEAGULL :				AVOID BIRD STRIKES!
