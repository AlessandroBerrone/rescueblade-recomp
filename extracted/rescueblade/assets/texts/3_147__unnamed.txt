MISSION 006 Nuclear Combustion
The local nuclear test facility is in serious trouble!
Can you avoid total meltdown?

Douse all the flames, and rescue the workers and scientists.

Lock down the burst steam vents with your water jet to get past them safely. Don't take too long!

Use the cableGuy to pull every reactor fuel rod up into the raised position, then douse the rod to cool it down.

Caution: Do not fly too close to the large power transformers!
