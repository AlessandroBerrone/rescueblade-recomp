MISSION 007 Ice Station
You have been sent on assignment to gain experience in the harsh extremes of Iceland. An SOS has been received from the local weather research station.

Your hose will not work in the sub zero temperatures. Find a novel way to extinguish the fires!

Use your down thrust to blow away snow drifts that have burried researchers. Then pull them to safety.

Use cableGuy to pull the comm towers back up into position whilst avoiding the escaped weather balloons!

WARNING: You must work at speed as there is ONLY ONE FUEL BARREL on this level!
