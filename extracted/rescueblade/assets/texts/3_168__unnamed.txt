MISSION 008 Sub Zero Sub
A foreign nuclear submarine has surfaced through the ice after experiencing electrical systems failure!

Rescue the sailors from the submarine. Unscrew each warhead then deliver it to the nuclear incident decontamination ship. Lower each warhead carefully into the ship's hold!

WARNING: Terrorists are trying to steal the nuclear warheads. Avoid their menacing black helicopters and dodge the ground to air missiles!

FIGHT BACK: Can you find a way to tilt the terrorists from their icebergs?
